<div class="page-header-line"></div>

<h2>Ongoing Projects</h2>

<div class="project-item">
  <h3>Ductile Failure under Dynamic and Complex Loading</h3>
  <p class="project-meta">
    <strong>Funding:</strong> ANRF
  </p>
  <p>
    Investigation of ductile deformation and failure of materials under
    complex and dynamic loading conditions, with emphasis on the roles
    of stress state, loading history, strain rate, and inertia.
    The project involves the development of computational approaches
    for linking microscale failure mechanisms with macroscopic material
    response.
  </p>
</div>


<div class="project-item">
  <h3>Mechanics of Battery Materials</h3>
  <p class="project-meta">
    <strong>Funding:</strong> Research Project
  </p>
  <p>
    Computational investigation of the coupled electrochemical and
    mechanical behaviour of battery electrode materials. The research
    focuses on deformation, stress development, damage, and failure
    arising from electrochemical processes during battery operation.
  </p>
</div>


<h2>Completed Projects</h2>

<div class="project-item">
  <h3>Ductile Failure in Low Triaxiality Stress Regime</h3>
  <p class="project-meta">
    <strong>Funding:</strong> IIT Indore YFRSG
  </p>
  <p>
    Computational study of ductile failure under low-triaxiality
    loading conditions, with emphasis on void evolution, material
    deformation, and the transition from distributed damage to
    localized failure.
  </p>
</div>
